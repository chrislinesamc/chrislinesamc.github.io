DHARANI

3D Developing Human-brain Atlas

Five fetal brains, 14–24 gestational weeks: 5,132 Nissl and H&E sections at 20 µm, postmortem MRI, and 466 annotated sections covering ~500 structures.

brainportal.humanbrain.in/publicview/index.htmlOpen →
ANCHOR

Atlas of Neurochemical Characterization of the Human Brainstem

More than 800 serial sections stained for Nissl and seven IHC markers, from brainstems at three ages (25 GW fetus, 9 and 54 years), with over 200 annotated structures.

anchor.humanbrain.inOpen →
C-Stroke

Cellular Ischemic Stroke Atlas of the Whole Human Brain

The first whole-brain, cell-resolution histological reconstruction of human ischemic stroke registered to clinical MRI; ~1,100 annotated, MRI-registered sections are open.
