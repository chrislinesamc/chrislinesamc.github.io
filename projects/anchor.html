Project · co-author

ANCHOR

The human brainstem across the lifespan, from a fetus to a child to an adult: an atlas of its neurochemistry, with 3D reconstruction.

Scroll

800+ serial sections

Each brainstem was cut across its length, section after section, and stained for Nissl and seven immunohistochemical markers. Here the sections close up into the whole.

25 weeks of gestation

Specimen 1, a fetal brainstem: 282 Nissl and 39 IHC sections, 78 of them annotated. In it the paper describes the pretectal nuclei.

9 years

Specimen 2: 120 Nissl, 530 IHC and 53 myelin sections, 91 of them annotated. Its viewer steps through the annotated sections alongside the matching IHC.

54 years

Specimen 3: 705 Nissl and 208 IHC sections, 33 of them annotated.

200+ structures

Identified and annotated by hand, then characterized with the seven IHC markers. The paper describes the catecholaminergic groups at all three ages, and the protoplasmic commissural dendrites of the hypoglossal nucleus.

From the block face to cells.

The brainstem here is built from block-face images: a photograph of the tissue block before every cut, stacked in 3D. The atlas aligns them with MRI and with the stained sections themselves.

ANCHOR: Atlas of Neurochemical Characterization of the Human Brainstem with 3D Reconstruction

Bota M, Venkatesh S, Arun Arunesh S, … Chrisline Sam C, … Verma R, Manger PR, Sivaprakasam M · all 24 authors Mihail Bota, Soundharya Venkatesh, Shevani Arun Arunesh, Nagajothi Ganesan, Supriti Mulay, Karan Ramana Gopi, Sruti Rekha Muni, Shrimathi Mani, Chrisline Sam C, A.S.T. Aditya Bharg, Vinoth Kanna G, Rajeswaran Rangaswami, S. Lata, E. Harish Kumar, S. Suresh, Mousumi Sen, Ranjit Immanuel James, Abi Manesh, George M. Varghese, K.V. Vinoth, Keerthi Ram, Richa Verma, Paul R. Manger, Mohanasankar Sivaprakasam
  • The most detailed human brainstem atlas across the lifespan: fetus, child and adult.
  • More than 800 serial sections stained for Nissl and seven immunohistochemical markers, with over 200 structures annotated and characterized with those markers.
  • Describes the catecholaminergic groups of the human brainstem across all three ages.
  • Identifies the protoplasmic commissural dendrites of the hypoglossal nucleus, and describes the pretectal nuclei in the 25-week fetal brainstem.
